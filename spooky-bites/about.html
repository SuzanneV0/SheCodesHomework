About Spooky Bites

A little kitchen corner of the web for Halloween baking.

What This Is

Spooky Bites is a small, non-commercial hobby site collecting Halloween baking ideas in one place: a short list of party bakes, a handful of full recipes, a techniques primer, and a not-very-serious quiz. It started as a sibling to Spookology, a general Halloween site, carved off so the baking material could have room to grow.

Who It's For

Home bakers who want their Halloween spread to look intentional without spending the whole of October on it. Most recipes here are make-ahead, most are forgiving, and the ones that aren't say so clearly. If you have made a boxed cake mix and owned a piping bag once, you are the target audience.

Why Marigold, Orange, and Purple

Halloween baking defaults to orange and black, which photographs as a muddy brown blur. Swapping black for a deep purple and adding marigold as a third colour gives a palette with actual contrast: purple for structure (the navigation, the footer), orange for headings and buttons, and marigold for the small bright accents — links, focus outlines, the glow on a card you're hovering. The techniques page goes into how to hit those same three colours in an actual bake without them turning grey.

The Fine Print

The newsletter and contact forms are front-end demos and don't send anything anywhere. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. See the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for the details, and get in touch if you have a recipe to share.
