Graveyard Dirt Cake

The make-ahead, no-bake dessert that feeds a crowd and looks like a tiny cemetery.

← All recipes

🕒 Prep 25 min ❄️ Chill 4 hr 🍽️ Serves 12–16 📊 Easy · no oven

Dirt cake is really a trifle in disguise: layers of cream-cheese-lightened chocolate pudding and crushed sandwich cookies, built in a single dish and decorated to look like freshly dug earth. It has to chill for at least four hours, which makes it the ideal thing to build the night before a party.

Ingredients

  • 1 x 510 g pack chocolate sandwich cookies
  • 115 g cream cheese, softened
  • 60 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 100 g icing sugar
  • 2 x 90 g boxes instant chocolate pudding mix
  • 720 ml cold whole milk
  • 250 g whipped topping or softly whipped double cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • To decorate: gummy worms, a few rectangular shortbread or Milano-style cookies, edible flowers or candy pumpkins

Method

  1. Blitz the sandwich cookies to fine crumbs in a food processor (or seal in a bag and crush with a rolling pin). Set aside about 250 g of crumbs for the top and middle layers.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, butter, and icing sugar together until completely smooth with no lumps.
  3. In a separate large bowl, whisk the pudding mix with the cold milk for 2 minutes, then let it stand 3 minutes until thick.
  4. Fold the cream cheese mixture into the pudding, then fold in the whipped topping and vanilla until no streaks remain.
  5. In a 23 x 33 cm dish, layer: half the remaining cookie crumbs, half the pudding, the reserved 250 g crumbs, then the rest of the pudding. Finish with the last of the crumbs so the top is solid "dirt."
  6. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.
  7. Just before serving, press the rectangular cookies in as tombstones, tuck gummy worms half-in / half-out of the dirt, and add flowers or candy pumpkins.

Make it spookier: write "RIP" on the tombstone cookies with a little melted white chocolate in a piped bag, and pipe a few extra worms out of the dirt with tinted buttercream. For individual servings, build it in clear plastic cups so the layers show.
