Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Do We Use Cookies?

No — not the browser kind. Spooky Bites does not set or read any cookies: no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, and no analytics cookies. You won't see a cookie consent banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to. (The recipe kind, we fully endorse.)

What We Keep in Local Storage Instead

A few small things are saved in your browser's local storage rather than in a cookie: your light/dark theme choice, your menu-planner checkboxes, and — after you subscribe to the newsletter or save a recipe to your favourites — the name and email you entered. Local storage differs from a cookie in a few important ways: it is not sent to a server with every page request, it stays only on the device and browser you set it on, and here it exists so the site can remember your preferences and show which recipes you have already favourited.

Your stored email is sent to the Spooky Bites database only when you actively save or remove a favourite, or subscribe. Use the "Not you?" link on the recipes page to wipe the stored name and email.

Third-Party Services

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and sends newsletter and favourite data to Supabase. Those services may use their own cookies or similar technology as part of serving their requests, governed by their own policies, not this site's. Spooky Bites itself reads no cookie from either.

Managing Local Storage

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time through your browser's settings (usually under "Clear browsing data" or "Site settings"). Doing so resets the theme toggle, empties the menu planner, and signs you out of favourites on this device. It does not delete rows already stored in the database.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change to reflect that.
