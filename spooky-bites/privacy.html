Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Short version: this is a student project. The newsletter and "save to favourites" features store the name, email address, and recipe choices you give them in a hosted database. Nothing else about you is collected. Please don't use an email address you would mind seeing in a class demo.

Newsletter Sign-up

The "Subscribe to our Newsletter" pop-up asks for your name and email address. When you submit it, those two fields are sent to a Supabase database (hosted PostgreSQL) and stored in a subscribers table so the list persists. There is no actual newsletter — no email is ever sent to you.

Saved Favourites

On the recipes pages you can save a recipe to your favourites. This records which recipe you saved against your email address in the same database (a favourites table). Saving a favourite also adds you to the subscribers table, because a favourite has to belong to someone. You can remove a favourite at any time, which deletes that row.

Data Stored in Your Browser

Your light/dark theme choice, your menu-planner checkboxes, and — once you have subscribed or saved a favourite — your name and email are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. The stored email lets the site show which recipes you have already favourited when you come back. Use the "Not you?" link on the recipes page to clear it. See our Cookie Policy for how local storage differs from a cookie.

Analytics & Tracking

This site does not run any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Beyond the subscriber and favourite records described above, we don't know who you are or what pages you've visited.

Third-Party Services

Page fonts load from Google Fonts, and the newsletter and favourites features talk to Supabase; both may receive standard technical request information (such as your IP address) as part of serving those requests. No other third-party service is used on this site. The Contact form is still a front-end-only demo and does not send anything anywhere.

Children's Privacy

This site is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change to reflect that.

Questions
